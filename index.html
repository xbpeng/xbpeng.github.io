Xue Bin (Jason) Peng

I'm an Assistant Professor at Simon Fraser University (SFU) and a Research Scientist at NVIDIA. I hold a Canada CIFAR AI Chair through Amii. I will be moving to the University of British Columbia (UBC) in 2027.

I received a Ph.D. from UC Berkeley, advised by Professor Sergey Levine and Professor Pieter Abbeel. Prior to that, I received an M.Sc from the University of British Columbia, advised by Professor Michiel van de Panne. My work lies in the intersection between computer graphics and machine learning, with a focus on reinforcement learning for motion control of simulated characters. I have previously worked for Sony, Google Brain, OpenAI, Adobe Research, Disney Research, Microsoft (343 Industries), and Capcom.
