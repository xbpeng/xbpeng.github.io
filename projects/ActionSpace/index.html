We compare the impact of four different action parameterizations (torques, muscle-activations, target joint angles, and target joint angle velocities) in terms of learning time, policy robustness, motion quality, and policy query rates. Our results are evaluated on a gait-cycle imitation task for multiple planar articulated figures and multiple gaits.
